This page lives on 127.0.0.1:3001, a different site from the bank on localhost:3000. It cannot read the bank's cookies. But it can make your browser send a request to the bank, and the browser may attach the bank's cookies on its own. That is CSRF (Cross-Site Request Forgery).

Attack 1: the unsafe endpoint

A hidden form that POSTs to /api/transfer-unsafe. That endpoint only checks the cookie.

Attack 2: the safe endpoint

Same trick against /api/transfer-safe. That endpoint also wants an X-CSRF-Token header. A plain HTML form can't add headers, and this site can't read the token, so the attack fails even if the cookie is sent.

What to expect

  • Logged in with SameSite=None: attack 1 works, attack 2 is blocked by the CSRF token.
  • Logged in with SameSite=Lax (the default) or Strict: the browser does not send the cookie on a cross-site POST, so both fail.
