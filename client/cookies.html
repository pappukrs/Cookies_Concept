Try these experiments

    
    
      
  1. HttpOnly: tick HttpOnly, click "Set from server". The cookie appears on the right but
        not on the left. That's why session ids should be HttpOnly: an XSS script can't steal them.

  2. Session vs persistent: clear Max-Age and set a cookie. Close the whole browser and come back:
        it's gone. (Some browsers keep session cookies if "continue where you left off" is on.)

  3. Expiry: set Max-Age to 5, wait 5 seconds, click Refresh. It disappears by itself.

        or the Console for a warning.
